# Senior Data Engineer, Delivery Intelligence (fictional example)

**Parcelwise** · Rotterdam, hybrid (2 days in office) · Full time

## About us
Parcelwise moves 1.2 million parcels a week for 3,000 online shops across the Benelux. We are a fast-paced, fun team that loves logistics and cares about our planet.

## What you'll do
- Own the pipelines that turn scanner events from 40 depots into the delivery-time estimates customers see at checkout.
- Bring our late-arriving event problem under control: today, depot scans can land up to 6 hours late and our ETA model trains on incomplete days.
- Move our batch jobs from cron scripts to Airflow, with data quality checks that fail loudly.
- Work with the ETA modelling team to define event-time features and backfill history safely.
- Mentor two mid-level engineers and review their dbt and Python code.

## What you bring
- 5+ years building production data pipelines in Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with streaming or late-arriving data (Kafka, event-time windows, watermarks).
- Strong dbt and Airflow experience in a cloud warehouse (we run BigQuery on GCP).
- You have owned data quality: tests, alerting, and the incident review afterwards.
- Clear written English; our docs and reviews are in English.

## Nice to have
- Experience with logistics, telematics or IoT event data.
- Familiarity with Terraform.
- Dutch is a plus, never a requirement.

## What we offer
- Salary €78k–€92k, 30 days holiday, NS business card.
- We sponsor visas for this role.
